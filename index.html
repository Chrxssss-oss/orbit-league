ORBIT LEAGUE

A zero-gravity VR team sport. Grab the walls, launch off your teammates, and throw the disc through the other team's hoop.

Join the test See it in action

How it plays

Two teams, one disc, no gravity. Every match starts with a launch out of the tubes and a race to the center of the arena.

01 / MOVE

Zero-g movement

Grab any surface and push off. Thrusters, a back booster and an air brake let you steer mid-flight.

02 / GRAB

Fight for the disc

Snatch the disc by its rim with your real hands. When two players grab it at once, the stronger pull wins the 50/50.

03 / THROW

Throw with your arm

The harder you throw, the faster it flies. Bank shots off the walls, pass to teammates, or headbutt it in.

04 / SCORE

2s and 3s

Score through the hoop for 2 points, or from outside the bubble for 3. Five-minute matches, highest score wins.

05 / LAUNCH

Launch tubes

After every goal, both teams reset in their launch rooms. Pick a tube, wait out the 15-second countdown, and fly.

06 / HANG OUT

Lobby and practice room

Queue for Quick Play, host a private match with a join code, or warm up on the practice hoops.

Controls

Built for Meta Quest Touch controllers. No teleporting, just your hands and some thrusters.

Supported headsets: Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S and Quest Pro.

GripGrab walls, players and the disc (hold it early to auto-grab)
Release + swingThrow the disc or push off a wall
B / YThrusters
Left stick clickBack booster (recharges every 4 seconds)
Right stick clickAir brake
Right stickSnap turn
